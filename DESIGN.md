# Design System: Rollins Bright Wonder Portfolio

## Overview

**Creative North Star: “The Field Journal Map.”**

The portfolio uses the language of a survey map: pale, walkable terrain; spare contour lines; a clearly marked purple course; and useful legend-like labels. It reflects a learning journey rather than pretending every project is complete. Visitors choose their own route through background, studies, interactive experiments, work in progress, and contact.

The page stays open and editorial instead of nesting every subject in a card. Engineering diagrams and measurement plots may use a fine grid when the grid explains the illustration. The route and facts remain readable without relying on motion or color alone.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Map-derived colors and contour linework.
- A purple course marks navigation and actionable links.
- Condensed headings contrast with readable prose and functional mono labels.
- Flat surfaces and open lists keep the focus on actual work and learning.

## Colors

The light theme takes its ground from field-map inks; forest and marsh greens describe the subject, while purple is reserved for the route and active navigation.

### Primary
- **Course Purple** (`#654696`): Main action, links, route points, and focus indicators.
- **Forest Green** (`#315b47`): Engineering and learning emphasis on light surfaces.

### Secondary
- **Deep Survey Green** (`#203d35`): Oscilloscope and experiment surfaces with light foreground text.
- **Marsh** (`#8eafaa`): Phase diagrams and supporting map details.
- **Open Land** (`#dce59e`): Waveform phase and measurement emphasis against dark green.
- **Skill markers** (`#65571c`, `#3d7478`): Small discipline identifiers; these shift to high-contrast field colors in the dark theme.

### Tertiary
- **Route Lilac** (`#cbb6ee`): Secondary phase line on the dark signal plot.
- **Earth Brown** (`#806b50`): The contour color for map-derived details.

### Neutral
- **Field** (`#e9eee4`): Main page background.
- **Ground** (`#f3f5ed`): Subtle inset and illustration surfaces.
- **Paper** (`#fbfcf6`): Light contrast surface.
- **Ink** (`#1b302d`): Main text.
- **Muted Ink** (`#4a6058`): Secondary text on light backgrounds.
- **Contour** (`#b7c0aa`): Rules and contour lines on light backgrounds.
- **Reverse ink** (`#fff`): Text on the light-theme purple course; dark-theme course controls use `#13231f` instead.

### Named Rules
**The Course Rule.** Purple identifies the visitor's route and actionable wayfinding; do not spread it across unrelated decoration.

**The Terrain Rule.** Topographic linework belongs to map surfaces. The engineering plot grid belongs to plots and circuit diagrams, not generic page backgrounds.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Barlow Condensed (with Arial Narrow, Impact, and sans-serif fallbacks)
**Body Font:** Archivo (with Segoe UI and Arial fallbacks)
**Label/Mono Font:** DM Mono (with Consolas fallback)

**Character:** Condensed display type gives the page a clear, map-title silhouette; Archivo keeps long descriptions comfortable to read. Mono is reserved for coordinates, measured values, and compact labels.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, `clamp(76px, 9.2vw, 132px)`, `.83` line height): Hero title.
- **Headline** (600, `clamp(54px, 7.3vw, 100px)`, `.96` line height): Section and contact titles.
- **Title** (600, `clamp(31px, 4.3vw, 52px)`): Project names.
- **Body** (400, `17px`, `1.65` line height): Introductory and narrative copy; constrain paragraphs to a readable measure.
- **Label** (400, `10px`): Control numbers, route descriptors, and technical metadata.

## Layout

The content uses a centered fluid frame, up to 1380px wide, with generous gaps between sections. At desktop widths, the hero pairs its message with the live signal plot; the route then connects the full-width sections. At 980px, dense two-column compositions relax; below 700px, the hero and experiment stack, project art moves above its description, and route stops become a horizontal scroll area.

Section labels follow their headings rather than forming eyebrow rows. Project entries use open ruled rows and varied diagrams, not a grid of identical cards.

## Elevation & Depth

The page is flat: no shadows or raised card surfaces. Distinction comes from map color roles, a dark measurement plot, and thin field rules.

## Shapes

Main content and action surfaces are mostly square-cornered. The circular RW mark and course controls borrow their form from map symbols. Keep borders and strokes fine and structural.

## Components

- **Navigation:** Compact inline links on wide screens; on small screens a button opens a stacked menu. Route links pair each mapped control number with a destination section and expose the current stop.
- **Course controls:** Open circle markers connected by a dashed route. Purple fill shows the current location or hover; preserve a visible focus outline.
- **Primary action:** Square-cornered purple field with white text; darken on hover and lift slightly.
- **Dark-theme action:** The purple route field lightens, so its label switches to dark reverse ink for readable contrast.
- **Signal plot:** Deep-green measurement field with labeled phase lines, grid, axis labels, and a speed slider. The canvas has an equivalent accessible description.
- **Experiment:** Forest-green control area with visible value labels and range endpoints, beside a dark readout area. The bulb responds to calculated power; current and power values stay readable.
- **Project entries:** Editorial rows with original diagram artwork, descriptive status, technologies, and a repository link only where a real repository exists.
- **Contact rows:** Show the existing public contact routes plainly; copy controls report success or explain recovery.
- **Theme control:** Toggles a complete light/dark palette and updates its accessible name and pressed state.

## Do's and Don'ts

- Do keep every profile, study, project, and contact claim grounded in the portfolio's real information.
- Do preserve the map distinction between fixed content and the visitor's route over it.
- Do keep map linework, plot grids, and mono labels tied to actual map or measurement content.
- Do make hover, focus, active, and current states distinguishable with keyboard access.
- Don't present in-progress notes or learning exercises as finished client work.
- Don't invent repositories, demos, customers, testimonials, or performance claims.
- Don't turn the open project list back into a wall of identical cards.
