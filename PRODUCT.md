# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

People visiting Rollins Bright Wonder's personal portfolio to learn about his electrical engineering studies, freelance web work, skills, and projects.

## Product Purpose

Present Rollins's background and current work in one responsive personal portfolio, and make it easy for visitors to explore projects or get in touch.

## Positioning

The portfolio brings electrical engineering (power option) studies and freelance web development together, alongside digital products and ongoing technical learning.

## Operating Context

The site is a static, no-build portfolio intended to run from a GitHub Pages repository. Visitors may arrive on desktop or mobile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single-page navigation to about, skills, interactive learning demonstrations, work, and contact.
- Keep the page responsive and usable without a build step.
- The displayed projects include work explicitly marked as in progress; do not present those as completed projects.
- Preserve the existing published email, phone/WhatsApp, LinkedIn, location, and GitHub contact routes unless Rollins changes them.

## Evidence on Hand

- Existing page copy and contact details in `index.html`.
- The existing repository is linked from the portfolio.
- Electrical study notes and the Python learning lab are described as in progress.
- Do not invent completed projects, clients, testimonials, credentials, or outcomes.

## Product Principles

- Make Rollins's engineering and web-development interests easy to understand.
- Show learning work honestly, including what is still in progress.
- Make project exploration and contact straightforward.
- Keep the static site simple to publish and maintain.
